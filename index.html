About

source → model → metric

I started as a data analyst building dashboards, and kept moving upstream to fix the problem at its source: messy, inconsistent data. Today most of my work happens in the layer between raw tables and reports, where I design data models, write tests, and define the metrics everyone else relies on.

At DAP Health I built a unified Epic clinical data model behind 60+ HEDIS and UDS quality measures. At Walmart I built dbt models in Snowflake that turned raw sales and inventory data into a governed star schema. On my own time I build end-to-end platforms with CDC, Kafka, Databricks, and Airflow to go deeper on the engineering side.

I hold an M.S. in Information Systems from California State University, Long Beach.
